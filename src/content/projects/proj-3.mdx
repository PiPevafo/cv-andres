Problem prepared for the International Physicists' Tournament (IPT), Universidad del Valle, Mar 2024. I analyzed how the peg size, peg distribution and impact angle of a Galton board modify the resulting probability distributions. Through theoretical analysis and C++ simulations, I showed that non-binomial and non-Gaussian distributions can emerge, leading to deviations from the conventional central limit theorem.
