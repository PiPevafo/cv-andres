Final project for the course *Nonlinear Dynamics and Quantum Chaos* (Universidad del Valle). Studied operator spreading in random Clifford circuits where CNOT gates act on a fraction $r$ of the bonds, using out-of-time-order correlators as a diagnostic of scrambling: early-time growth, saturation around the scrambling time and the dependence on $r$, simulated with Qiskit for 129 qubits.
