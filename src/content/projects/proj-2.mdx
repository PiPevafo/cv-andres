Co-organizer and mentor of the Qiskit Fall Fest 2025 at Universidad del Valle (Cali, Nov 2025), bringing together students and researchers for talks, workshops, a hackathon and hands-on sessions on quantum computing. With Nicolás Aguilera I gave a talk on classical vs quantum optimization, based on the knapsack problem and QAOA.
