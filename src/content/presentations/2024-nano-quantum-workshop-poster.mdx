A graph-theory-inspired algorithm computes the minimal cut of random local circuits with one- and two-qubit gates, giving the Hartley entropy $S_0$ as an upper bound for all Rényi entropies. The poster compares this geometric bound with exact entropies and derives the thermodynamic limit of entanglement growth in qubit number and depth.
