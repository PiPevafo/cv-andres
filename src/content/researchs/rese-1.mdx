**Quantic Group, Universidad del Valle · Jan 2024 – Jun 2025.** Advisors: Javier Madroñero, PhD; William Esteban Salazar (CQT, National University of Singapore).

Developed a novel and efficient graph-based algorithm, grounded in tensor-network representations of local quantum circuits, for estimating bipartite entanglement entropies and analyzing architecture-dependent saturation. Using the minimal-cut formalism, I derived classical upper bounds and established a system-size-dependent entanglement velocity, $v_E \sim 1/2L$, confirmed analytically and numerically with KPZ scaling.

The framework was extended to doped circuits with three-qubit non-Clifford gates, obtaining analytical lower bounds and characterizing deviations in universality and non-stabilizer resource generation. A paper is under construction.

Distinguished as a *Meritorious Thesis* by the Faculty Council of Universidad del Valle (2026).
