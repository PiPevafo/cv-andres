**GERAD Group, Université du Québec à Montréal (UQAM) · Jun – Aug 2025.** Advisor: Franklin Djeumou Fomeni, PhD.

Developed a feasibility-preserving quantum optimization framework for the Quadratic Knapsack Problem by integrating QAOA with the Quantum Tree Generator, so that the evolution stays strictly within the feasible subspace without penalty terms. Benchmarked against greedy heuristics and classical solvers, achieving competitive performance at high densities. Extended the approach to the Densest Subgraph problem with negative weights using XY-mixers to analyze performance–cost trade-offs.
