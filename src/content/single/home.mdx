I am a physicist working on quantum information and quantum many-body physics. I completed my B.Sc. in Physics at Universidad del Valle (Cali) in 2025 as the best graduating student of my class (GPA 4.41/5.0), with a thesis distinguished as *Meritorious*, and since the second semester of 2026 I am an M.Sc. student at Universidad de los Andes (Bogotá).

My research revolves around four connected themes:

- **Quantum magic (nonstabilizerness) and majorization** *(current, master's)*: characterizing and comparing magic states, in particular entangled ones, through majorization.
- **Entanglement structure of quantum circuits**: tensor-network representations, minimal-cut bounds and entanglement growth in local circuits, including doped circuits with non-Clifford gates.
- **Quantum algorithms and optimization**: variational and constraint-preserving algorithms (QAOA, quantum tree generators) for combinatorial and financial problems.
- **Classical simulation and resource estimation**: tensor-network methods and non-stabilizer resources as a measure of what NISQ-era circuits can and cannot do.

This site collects my [research](/cv-andres/research), [publications](/cv-andres/publications), [posters and slides](/cv-andres/presentations) and [projects](/cv-andres/projects).
