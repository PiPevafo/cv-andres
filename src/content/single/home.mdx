# {frontmatter.title}

*MSc student in Physics, Universidad de los Andes (Bogotá, Colombia) · Qiskit Advocate*

I am a physicist working on quantum information and quantum many-body physics. I completed my B.Sc. in Physics at Universidad del Valle (Cali) in 2025, graduating first in my class (GPA 4.41/5.0) with a meritorious thesis, and I am now a master's student at Universidad de los Andes.

My research revolves around three connected themes:

- **Entanglement structure of quantum circuits**: tensor-network representations, minimal-cut bounds and entanglement growth in local circuits, including doped circuits with non-Clifford gates.
- **Quantum algorithms and optimization**: variational and constraint-preserving algorithms (QAOA, quantum tree generators) for combinatorial and financial problems.
- **Classical simulation and resource estimation**: tensor-network methods and non-stabilizer resources as a measure of what NISQ-era circuits can and cannot do.

This site collects my [research](./research), [publications](./publications), [posters and slides](./presentations) and [projects](./projects).
